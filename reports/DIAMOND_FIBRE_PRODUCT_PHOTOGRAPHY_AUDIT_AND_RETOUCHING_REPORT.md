## 1. Executive Summary & Art Direction Philosophy

Diamond Fibre manufactures authentic handcrafted mattresses (gaddas), pillows, traditional razais, and plush mink blankets directly from its Ratlam workshop. While the raw photographs captured genuine products with authentic craftsmanship, the original files suffered from severe commercial presentation defects:
- **Intrusions**: Accidental capture of human feet in white socks, standing persons, room door frames/latches, and furniture.
- **Excessive Floor Clutter**: Uncleaned concrete and tile floors occupying 35%–65% of the frame, with dust, grout lines, and stains distracting from the bedding.
- **Rotation & Angle Errors**: Several key products photographed rotated sideways by 90 degrees or with severe perspective distortion.
- **Color Casts & Flat Lighting**: Ambient fluorescent lighting and cool floor reflections washing out the rich fabrics.

### Art Direction Principles
1. **Absolute Product Truthfulness**: Zero artificial fabric replacement, AI hallucination, or synthetic rendering. All product textures, hand-tufting rosettes, piped borders, and print patterns remain 100% authentic Diamond Fibre manufactured goods.
2. **Re-framing & Focal Isolation**: Eliminated all extraneous floor areas, feet, door latches, and background furniture. Re-oriented sideways images to natural horizontal/vertical bedspread presentations.
3. **Brand Palette Alignment**: Cleaned floor boundaries and adjusted midtones to align with Diamond Fibre's signature Warm Ivory (`#F3EFE7`/`#FAF8F4`), Sand (`#D9CCB6`), and Terracotta (`#E36E48`) palette, preserving natural contact shadows.
4. **Color Calibration & Tactile Clarity**: Corrected white balance, warmed midtones, and enhanced fabric micro-contrast so jacquard, brocade, velvet, and fleece textures look rich, plush, and tactile without artificial plastic-like effects.
5. **Non-Destructive Workflow**: 100% of original source photos are preserved intact in `public/images/`. Retouched derivatives are stored in `public/images/retouched/`.
